One of the most common questions from first-time real estate investors in Pune is: **"Should I buy a 2 BHK or a 3 BHK?"** The answer, as with most real estate decisions, depends entirely on your investment horizon, financial profile, and target tenant demographic. This guide provides a data-driven framework to help you decide.

## The Numbers: 2 BHK vs 3 BHK in PCMC (2026)

| Metric | 2 BHK | 3 BHK |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Starting Price (PCMC Premium) | ₹1.20 Crore | ₹1.75 Crore |
| Monthly EMI (20Y, 8.5%) | ~₹83,000 | ~₹1,21,000 |
| Monthly Rental (Premium Township) | ₹32,000–42,000 | ₹48,000–65,000 |
| Gross Rental Yield | 3.8–4.7% | 3.6–4.9% |
| Target Tenant | Young couples, singles | Families, senior IT professionals |
| Resale Liquidity | Very High | High |

## The Case for 2 BHK

A **2 BHK in a premium sports township** is the workhouse of real estate investment. The lower entry price means lower EMI, faster loan repayment, and broader tenant eligibility. In Pune's IT corridor, young couples earning combined salaries of ₹15–20 LPA are the most active rental demographic, and they systematically target well-maintained 2 BHK units in gated communities.

**The 2 BHK advantage:** If your goal is maximum rental yield with minimum vacancy periods, a 2 BHK in a high-amenity township is the optimal choice.

## The Case for 3 BHK

A **3 BHK unit** in a premium project is an aspirational asset. It attracts a higher-income, longer-tenure tenant: senior IT managers, CXOs, and family units who sign 2-3 year leases. The resulting reduction in vacancy periods and tenant churn can add 15-20% to effective annual yield versus a 2 BHK.

Moreover, the resale market for 3 BHK units in sports townships is distinctly thin — there simply aren't many of them. Scarcity creates pricing power, and 3 BHK units in managed townships like **The Arena** have historically commanded a 20-25% premium above the general market on resale.

**The 3 BHK advantage:** If your horizon is 5-7 years and you can comfortably service the higher EMI, a 3 BHK in a premium township will likely outperform the 2 BHK on total return.

## The Verdict for 2026

Given current market conditions in PCMC and the Hinjewadi IT corridor, our analysis suggests:

- **First-time investor, budget-conscious:** → **2 BHK at ₹1.20 Crore**
- **Established investor, 5+ year horizon:** → **3 BHK at ₹1.75 Crore**
- **NRI investor seeking rental yield + appreciation:** → **3 BHK** (higher ticket, lower management headache, longer leases)

Both configurations are available at **Mahalaxmi The Arena** and **Kohinoor The Arena** — Pune's first ILESEUM-managed sports township in Pimpri.
