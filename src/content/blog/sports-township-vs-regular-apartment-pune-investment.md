A new category of real estate has emerged in Pune's premium residential market — the **Sports Township**. The central question for investors is: does the sports infrastructure premium translate to measurable financial returns?

## What Makes a Sports Township Different?

A conventional gated community offers a swimming pool, a gym, and perhaps a badminton court. This is now table stakes — every project from ₹60 Lakhs upward offers this.

A **Sports Township** is a fundamentally different asset class. The Arena's 80,000 Sq.Ft. sports ecosystem — managed professionally by ILESEUM Sports Management — includes:
- Full cricket ground with practice nets
- Professional tennis academy
- Olympic-standard swimming pool
- Basketball and futsal courts
- A fully equipped sports performance centre
- Child development and youth sports programmes

The difference isn't just square footage. It's the **managed operations model**. ILESEUM operates the sports infrastructure as a professional facility — with coaches, scheduling systems, and performance tracking — rather than as passive common area. This means residents get genuine value, not window-dressing.

| Premium gated community | ₹35,000 – ₹45,000 | 5-8% | 3.8 – 4.2% |
| **Sports Township (The Arena)** | **₹45,000 – ₹58,000** | **2-4%** | **4.5 – 5.2%** |

The sports township premium on rent is **28-35% above** a standard premium gated community. The reason is demographic: IT professionals and senior executives with families actively seek sports infrastructure for their children. When they find it, they sign longer leases and churn far less frequently.

**For end-users:** A sports township delivers a measurably better quality of life for families with children. The health and wellness infrastructure creates daily value that a standard gym cannot.

**For investors:** The rental premium, lower vacancy, and superior capital appreciation make a sports township the highest risk-adjusted real estate investment in PCMC's current market.
