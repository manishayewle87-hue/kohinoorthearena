## FEMA Regulations — What Every NRI Must Know

Under India's **Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA)**, NRIs holding Indian passports or OCI cardholders can freely purchase any number of residential or commercial properties in India without requiring any RBI approval, with one key exception: agricultural land, plantation property, and farmhouses.

**Repatriation Rules:**
- You can repatriate up to **USD 1 million per financial year** from the sale of property
- Funds must be routed through an NRE or NRO bank account
- Capital gains tax applies: **20% with indexation** on long-term gains (held > 24 months)

## MahaRERA — The NRI's Non-Negotiable Due Diligence Checklist

Before committing to any project in Maharashtra, verify the following at [maharera.mahaonline.gov.in](https://maharera.mahaonline.gov.in):

**Verify: Registration Number, Project Completion Date, Promoter's Track Record, Escrow Account Compliance**

A MahaRERA-registered project is legally bound to deposit 70% of buyer payments into a dedicated escrow account, which can only be used for construction. This single regulation has dramatically reduced the risk of project delays for NRI investors.
